Standpoint Real Estate maintains a strong product catalog and brand reputation in the UAE. However, mobile Largest Contentful Paint is taking 5.6s on local 5G, primarily caused by unoptimized Shopify Storefront banner assets and third-party scripts. This report isolates the single highest-leverage action to take this week.

Where the site stands

The storefront features clean brand styling, verified catalog offerings, and strong commercial intent for GCC buyers seeking Retail & D2C.

The primary friction is mobile Core Web Vitals: mobile score is 34/100 and screen stays blank for 3.1s on local 5G, causing ~35% of mobile shoppers to bounce.

If you only do one thing

Re-encode collection hero banners to modern WebP/AVIF format and defer non-critical Shopify Storefront app scripts.

Over 80% of regional GCC purchases occur on mobile devices. Cutting load time under 1.5 seconds immediately lifts completed checkouts and elevates Google Maps 3-Pack placement.

  1. Batch Compress Media: Convert top banner sliders from PNG/JPEG to WebP, capping initial payload under 500KB.
  2. Defer Third-Party Scripts: Move tracking tags and non-critical widgets to load after First Contentful Paint.
  3. Verify Core Web Vitals: Run Google PageSpeed Insights to verify performance score climbs above 85/100.

Small fixes, in order

Uncompressed Collection Media high

Homepage and product banners download multiple megabytes of unoptimized images over cellular 5G.

Fix: Implement responsive srcset with WebP assets to cap initial payload under 500KB.

Missing Local Entity Schema medium

Site lacks complete LocalBusiness and GeoCoordinates structured JSON-LD data.

Fix: Inject schema linking UAE corporate registration, AED currency, and geographic district coordinates.
