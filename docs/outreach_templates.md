# UAE B2B Outreach Scripts & Personalization Framework

> **Guiding Principle**: Value-First, Highly Personalized, Zero Spam.  
> Every message addresses a specific, observable bottleneck and offers immediate, actionable value rather than a generic sales pitch.

---

## 1. WhatsApp Outreach (Fastest UAE B2B Response)

*Use when contacting the public business WhatsApp number listed on their website or Google Maps.*

```text
I recorded a quick 90-second screen teardown showing 2 quick fixes that could instantly cut your mobile load time and add a 1-click WhatsApp lead booking button.

Would you like me to send over the link here? No obligation at all.

Best,
[Your Name]
ApexFlow Digital | UAE Digital Growth Partner
```

## 4. Discovery Phone Call Script (Warm Inbound / Follow-Up)

*Duration: 10–15 minutes*

1. **Rapport & Context (1 min)**:
   > "Hi [Name], thanks for taking the call. I know you're busy running [Company Name], so I'll be brief. I wanted to quickly review the growth audit findings we shared regarding your website speed and WhatsApp lead pipeline."
2. **Current State Diagnosis (3 mins)**:
   > "How are you currently capturing and qualifying leads from your website and social campaigns? Are they being manually entered into a spreadsheet, or do you have an automated CRM bridge?"
3. **Quantifying Pain / Cost of Inaction (3 mins)**:
   > "When a prospect reaches out on your website after hours or on weekends, what is the average response time before someone follows up? In the UAE, response times over 15 minutes cut close rates by half."
4. **Presenting the Solution & Outcome (3 mins)**:
   > "What we do at ApexFlow is build a high-speed web experience with an automated webhook bridge that routes every inquiry to WhatsApp and your CRM in under 30 seconds. This typically frees up 15+ team hours a week and doubles conversion."
5. **Call-To-Action / Next Step (2 mins)**:
   > "I'd love to prepare a specific proposal and scope for [Company Name]. Would Tuesday at 11 AM or Wednesday at 2 PM work for a 20-minute screen-share roadmap session?"
