## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1. The Service Provider shall perform the digital services described in **Schedule A (Statement of Work)** attached hereto, including but not limited to:
* Website Design & Development
* Search Engine Optimization (SEO) & Technical Marketing
* AI & Business Workflow Automation (Webhooks, CRM sync, WhatsApp integrations)

1.2. Any additional requests, features, or out-of-scope work requested by the Client shall be documented in a separate written Change Order and billed at the agreed hourly rate of **AED [350] / hour** or a mutually agreed fixed sum.

---

## 2. COMPENSATION & PAYMENT TERMS

2.1. **Project Fees**: The Client agrees to pay the Service Provider a total fee of **AED [Amount in AED]** (exclusive of VAT, if applicable).

2.2. **Payment Schedule**:
* **Deposit**: 50% upon signing of this Agreement prior to project kickoff.
* **Midpoint Milestone**: 25% upon delivery and approval of initial design / staging prototype.
* **Final Milestone**: 25% upon final QA testing and immediately prior to live production deployment / domain DNS handover.

2.3. **Payment Method**: Payments shall be remitted via UAE Local Bank Transfer (IBAN) in UAE Dirhams (AED) or credit card via official payment gateway.

2.4. **Late Payment**: Invoices overdue by more than 14 calendar days shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted under UAE law.

---

## 3. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES & DELAYS

3.1. The Client agrees to provide all necessary assets (logos, branding guidelines, content copy, access credentials, API keys) within **7 business days** of request.

3.2. Project timelines are conditional upon timely Client feedback. Failure to provide feedback within 5 business days will automatically extend project delivery milestones accordingly.

---

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & OWNERSHIP

4.1. **Assignment**: Upon receipt of **100% full and final payment**, the Service Provider assigns and transfers to the Client all proprietary rights, title, and interest in the custom deliverables created specifically for the Client under this Agreement.

4.2. **Pre-Existing IP & Tools**: The Service Provider retains ownership of its proprietary frameworks, boilerplate code, reusable automation modules, scripts, and pre-existing intellectual property.

4.3. **Portfolio Rights**: The Service Provider retains the non-exclusive right to showcase the completed work, project screenshots, and non-confidential case study metrics in its professional portfolio and marketing materials.

---

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-DISCLOSURE (NDA)

5.1. Both Parties agree to hold all proprietary information, business data, customer lists, and financial figures disclosed during the engagement in strict confidence.

5.2. Confidential Information shall not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent, except where required by UAE law or official regulatory authorities.

---

## 6. WARRANTIES & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1. **30-Day Post-Launch Warranty**: The Service Provider provides a thirty (30) day warranty post-launch to fix any verifiable functional bugs or errors within the original agreed scope at zero additional charge.

6.2. **Limitation of Liability**: To the maximum extent permitted by UAE law, the Service Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client under this Agreement.

---

## 7. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION

7.1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the federal laws of the **United Arab Emirates** and the local laws applicable in the Emirate of **Dubai**.

7.2. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be resolved amicably through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Dubai (or the Small Claims Tribunal of the DIFC Courts, where elected).
